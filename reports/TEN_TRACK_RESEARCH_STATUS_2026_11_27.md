## Claims supported now

The project has constructed authentic sequence and chromatin inputs, executed a leakage-controlled developmental prediction comparison, and quantified which source representations can alter constrained graph edits. BIO-V8 confirms decision influence and computational preservation; it does not establish that the changed decisions improve predictions or biological accuracy. The BIO-V7 primary result still supplies no established incremental predictive advantage for GCEV. Predicted source weights are not biological probabilities.

The 10,000-resample paired-cell intervals describe uncertainty conditional on one fitted cohort and its fixed gene set. They are not biological-replicate intervals. The 56 method–condition combinations are repeated analyses of the same cohort, not 56 independent experiments.
