## What changed in this phase

The manuscript's substantive text and numerical presentation are complete. The full saved synthetic metric exports were recovered, preserving all eleven methods in the first study, the later thirteen-method parent ablation, precision at seven, direction ties and cost accounting. Their inclusion prevents omission of prespecified secondary endpoints. Descriptive summaries were assembled from those saved outputs; no model was refitted, confidence interval replaced, hypothesis test added or sealed biological outcome opened.

The evidence ledger records the exact bytes of the local source reports, protocols, saved result summaries and recovered metric tables. It excludes expression matrices, raw assay payloads, individual test-cell observations and generating edge/ranking payloads. Its claim map distinguishes source availability, action influence, predictive error, synthetic edge recovery and biological validation.

Writing progress does not close scientific gates. The three synthetic primary tests remain unsuccessful, and the BIO-V7 primary comparison does not establish incremental predictive benefit. Fixed source weighting is stronger on clean synthetic AP; some secondary AP and later NMSE comparisons favor GCEV and remain reported with their original interpretation. Computational reproduction adds confidence in the executed arithmetic, not independent experimental units.

## Claims supported now

The completed work supports a controlled evaluation of the implemented one-step source-utility procedure and a diagnosis of its source-use behavior. A positive learned source weight need not mean that the represented source can alter the action; a changed action need not improve prediction. Target-constant additive accessibility is invariant for within-target TF selection, while multiplicative representations can change selections without demonstrated predictive or biological benefit.

The synthetic studies use small generated networks and truth-derived priors. Their 112 disjoint seeded realizations are not 112 biological systems or a pooled confirmatory estimate. The K562 intervals condition on one fitted cohort and fixed gene universe; the 56 method–condition outputs are repeated analyses, not 56 independent experiments. No real regulatory AP, AUROC, F1, direct-regulation claim or RL superiority is established.
